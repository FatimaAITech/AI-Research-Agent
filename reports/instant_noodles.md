# Introduction
Instant noodles, also known as instant ramen, have become a staple food in many parts of the world. They are a type of pre-cooked and dried noodle block, often accompanied by flavoring powder and/or seasoning oil. The convenience and affordability of instant noodles have made them a popular choice among consumers. This report aims to provide an overview of instant noodles, covering their definition, history, types, production process, nutritional content, market trends, environmental concerns, and future developments.

## Definition and Classification
Instant noodles are defined as a type of food consisting of noodles sold in a pre-cooked and dried block with flavoring powder and/or seasoning oil. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instant_noodles), the dried noodle block was originally created by flash-frying cooked noodles, which is still the dominant method used in Asian countries. Instant noodles can be classified into different types based on their ingredients, cooking methods, and flavor profiles.

The production process of instant noodles involves cooking and drying noodles, followed by packaging with flavoring powder and/or seasoning oil. The dominant method used in Asian countries is flash-frying cooked noodles, which helps to remove excess moisture and preserve the noodles.
