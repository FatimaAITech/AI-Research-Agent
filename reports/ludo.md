## Rules and Gameplay
The rules of Ludo are simple: each player has four tokens that must be moved from the starting point to the finishing point, according to the rolls of a single die. As mentioned on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo), the game is a strategy-based board game that requires players to make tactical decisions to win. The gameplay involves rolling the die, moving tokens, and trying to reach the finishing point before other players.
