## Definition and Overview

## History of Buldak Noodles
The concept of Buldak noodles originated from a traditional Korean dish called "Buldak," which consists of chicken cooked in a spicy gochujang sauce. Inspired by this dish, Samyang Foods created the instant noodle version, called "Fire Chicken Stir Fried Noodles" or "Buldak-bokkeum-myeon." As mentioned in [Source 2](https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/recipes/buldak-ramen), this product became a huge success and helped establish Samyang Foods as a leading manufacturer of instant noodles.
