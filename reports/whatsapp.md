## Definition and Overview
WhatsApp is a free messaging and calling app that allows users to stay in touch with friends and family worldwide. ## Security and Privacy Concerns
WhatsApp prioritizes security and privacy, offering end-to-end encryption for all messages and calls. This means that only the sender and recipient can read or listen to the messages and calls, making it a secure platform for communication. The app also allows users to control who can see their online status, profile picture, and other personal information.

## Business and Marketing Applications
WhatsApp offers various business solutions, including the WhatsApp Business App and WhatsApp Business API. The WhatsApp Business App is designed for small businesses, allowing them to personally manage conversations with customers. The WhatsApp Business API, on the other hand, is suitable for medium to large businesses, providing programmatic access to communicate with customers at scale.
