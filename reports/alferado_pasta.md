## Definition and Origin
Fettuccine Alfredo is a pasta dish consisting of fettuccine tossed with butter and Parmesan cheese, which is melted and emulsified to form a rich cheese sauce coating the pasta. According to [Source 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fettuccine_Alfredo), it originated in Rome in the early 20th century.

## History of Alferado Pasta
The history of Alferado pasta is closely tied to its creator, Alfredo Di Lelio, who first introduced the dish in his restaurant in Rome. Over time, the recipe has evolved, and various adaptations have been made.
