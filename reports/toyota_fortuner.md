# Introduction
The Toyota Fortuner is a mid-size SUV manufactured by the Japanese automaker Toyota. It has been in production since 2004 and is built on the Hilux pickup truck platform. The Fortuner is available in either rear-wheel drive or four-wheel drive configuration and features two or three rows of seats. This report aims to provide an in-depth analysis of the Toyota Fortuner, including its history, design and features, engine and performance, models and variants, safety and technology, market analysis, competitor comparison, and customer reviews.

The Toyota Fortuner was first introduced in 2004 and has since become a popular SUV in many countries. ## Engine and Performance
The Toyota Fortuner is available in both manual and automatic transmission. The SUV is powered by a 4-cylinder, in-line, 16-valve DOHC variable nozzle turbo with air-cooled intercooler. The engine is capable of producing a significant amount of power, making it suitable for both on-road and off-road driving. The Fortuner also features a double wishbone suspension in the front and multi-link suspension in the rear, providing a smooth ride and stable handling.
