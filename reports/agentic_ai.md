## Definition and Characteristics
Agentic AI is defined as an autonomous AI system that can act independently to achieve specific goals with limited supervision. According to [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/agentic-ai), agentic AI consists of AI agents—machine learning models that mimic human decision-making. These agents can perform complex tasks, such as multitasking, and make decisions based on data analysis. As explained by [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/agentic-ai), agentic AI systems are proactive and can perform tasks autonomously.
